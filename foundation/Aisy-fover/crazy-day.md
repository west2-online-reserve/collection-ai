## 代码

这个代码可用于人民币和美元之间的转换💰
```
money=input()
if money[-1] in ['$']:
    rmb=eval(money[:-1])*6.78
    print("{:.2f}￥".format(rmb))
elif money[-3:] in ['USD']:
    rmb=eval(money[:-3])*6.78
    print("{:.2f}RMB".format(rmb))
elif money[-1] in ['￥']:
    usd=(eval(money[:-1]))/6.78
    print("{:.2f}$".format(usd))
elif money[-3:] in ['RMB']:
    usd=eval(money[:-3])/6.78
    print("{:.2f}USD".format(usd))
```
